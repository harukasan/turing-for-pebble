Colormap palettes
=================

The viridis, magma, plasma, inferno, cividis, and turbo palettes in
lib/palettes.ts and the generated core/palettes.h are eight samples, the
entries round(i * 255 / 7) for i = 0 to 7, of the 256-entry tables in
matplotlib's lib/matplotlib/_cm_listed.py at commit
2d0352ae25bd3be72b1585f70e36b5e4cd07bc9c
(https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib), scaled to 8-bit channels.
The colormaps originate from the following works.
